

Image taking process for specimens on the LAXCO 3000 Microscope at the TENN herbarium

By Julian Broughton 
Last Updated 3 July 2023


1. Make sure that specimens are hydrated before capturing images
2. Check to see if the camera is screwed onto the microscope, plugged into the computer, and turned on (the on off switch is at the back of the camera).
3. Turn on the computer and open the LAXCO application on the desktop home screen (Should be a gray and red X logo).
4. Once you are logged into the software, you will see two buttons in the bottom left corner of the screen, the pause/play button starts and stops the live footage coming from the connected camera. The other button is what you will use to capture your images.
5. Set up your specimen plate on the microscope and check that it is visible to the camera by clicking the pause/play button on the bottom left hand side of the screen.
6. Use the magnification turret on the microscope to choose the lens that is best for the desired image.
7. After choosing the desired magnification and aligning the image, use the stamp icon on the bottom left portion of the screen to capture the image that is on the screen. Once you click this button, the system will ask you want magnification is being used and make sure that you choose the same magnification that is on the turret.
8. After capturing the image, a folder will show in the top left corner and it will have every image you have taken during this session. To export your images to the server or computer, right click on the images in this folder and export them into the desired folder.


Types of images that need to be taken:
Apex (APX): The uppermost point of the moss leaf (Usually taken on 40x)
Margin (MGN): The border of the edge of the apical portion of the leaf. (Usually taken on 40x)
Vaginant Lamina (VL): A close look at the cells that are in the middle of the vaginant portion (side to side) of the leaf (Usually taken on 40x)
Mid Dorsal Lamina (MDL): A look at the opposite side of the vaginant portion of the leaf (Usually taken on 40x)
Cross Section (CX): Samples where the specimen was sliced across a cell
Apex cross section (AX): Same as cross section but this slice is higher and does not show the vaginant lamina.
